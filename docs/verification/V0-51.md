Section: docs/rfcs/v0.md#15-watchers-and-approvals

## Environment
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Host: owner's Mac, Darwin 25.6.0 (arm64)
- Toolchain: cargo 1.89.0
- Network: none

## Notes
`Watchers::add_schedule` stores a cron and prompt. `tick(now, log)` finds each enabled schedule's
next cron occurrence after `last_fired` and, if it is at or before `now`, appends the prompt to
the schedule thread as a `turn.user` event (as if the owner typed it) and advances `last_fired`.
Test `schedule_fires_at_cron_time` uses a controlled clock: a per-minute schedule does not fire
at 30 s, fires once past the 60 s boundary, lands the prompt in the schedule thread, and does not
refire on a repeat tick.
