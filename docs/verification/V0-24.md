## Notes
When the handoff flag is set, `run_talk` finishes the current chunk (its sentence), emits
`talk.handoff` and returns without a `talk.done`. Test `think_verdict_makes_talk_finish_and_hand_off`
asserts the event order: at least one `talk.delta`, then `talk.handoff`, and no `talk.done`.
