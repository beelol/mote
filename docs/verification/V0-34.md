## Notes
`mem::retrieve` takes the FTS top 20 and the sqlite-vec vector top 20, adds their bounded 2-hop
neighbours (<= 200 edges/node), fuses by reciprocal rank (k=60) with a 30-day recency half-life,
excludes superseded nodes, and cuts to the limit. Test `retrieve_p50_under_40ms_on_10k_nodes`
builds a 10k-node fixture with edges and measures retrieve p50 over 30 runs.

## Result
